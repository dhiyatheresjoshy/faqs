Frequently Asked Questions

General

  • What is this website about?
    This website is designed to help users learn web development through tutorials, projects, and community support.

  • Who can use this website?
    Anyone interested in learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and frontend development can use this website.

  • Is the content free?
    Yes, most of the tutorials and resources are completely free to access.

Account & Login

  • Do I need to create an account to access content?
    No, you can browse the tutorials without signing up. However, creating an account gives access to progress tracking and quizzes.

  • I forgot my password. What should I do?
    Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page, and you’ll receive a password reset link via email.

  • Can I delete my account?
    Yes, go to your profile settings and click “Delete Account.” Please note this action is irreversible.

Learning & Courses

  • What technologies do you cover?
    We cover HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Git, and basic design principles.

  • Are there projects I can build?
    Each course includes hands-on projects like to-do apps, responsive websites, and portfolio pages.

  • How long does it take to finish a course?
    It depends on your pace, but most courses can be completed in 2–4 weeks with regular practice.

Support & Contact

  • How can I get help if I'm stuck?
    You can post your questions in our discussion forum or join our Discord community for live help.

  • Is there a way to contact support directly?
    Yes, use the “Contact Us” form or email support@example.com for assistance.

  • Do you have a feedback system?
    Absolutely! Each tutorial has a feedback form so you can rate and suggest improvements.
